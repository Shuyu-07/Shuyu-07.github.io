Greetings and Welcome to My Homepage

Biography

I am currently an Assistant Research Fellow at Sichuan University (SCU) and a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Cyber Security Research Centre (CYSREN), Nanyang Technological University (NTU). My postdoctoral research is jointly supervised by Prof. Tianwei Zhang at NTU and Prof. Yi Zhang at SCU. I received my Ph.D. and B.E. degrees from Sichuan University in 2025 and 2019, respectively, under the supervision of Prof. Xingshu Chen.

My research focuses on the security and trustworthiness of AI systems, with particular interests in LLM safety guardrails, security and reliability of AI agents, and AI interpretability. I aim to understand and mitigate security risks in increasingly autonomous AI systems, while exploring how AI is reshaping online social networks. I am always excited to explore new research problems and welcome opportunities for academic collaboration.
