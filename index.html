About Me

I am an Assistant Research Fellow at Sichuan University and a Visiting Scholar at the Cyber Security Research Centre @ NTU (CYSREN). I received my Ph.D. from Sichuan University under the supervision of Prof. Xingshu Chen. My postdoctoral research is supervised by Prof. Tianwei Zhang and Prof. Yi Zhang.

Research Interests

Large language model security; AI agent security; social network analysis; open-source intelligence.

Education

  • B.E. in Information Security, Sichuan University, 2015–2019
